## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Shark invaded a ship. The Captain must collect specific items to defeat the invading shark and save his ship.

**Storyline:**

A shark has invaded the ship. As the newly appointed captain, you must explore the ship, collect specific items, and use them strategically to defeat the invading shark. You will need defensive manual guide from the **Bridge Room** to learn how to defeat the shark, Harpoon gun from the **Mechanical Room** to use as primary weapon for defending, Fishing net  from **Mess Room** to trap the shark temporarily, Flashlight  from **Supply Room** to help navigate dark areas of the ship, First-Aid kit  from **Cabin** **Room** to recover if injured, and finally Energy drink from the **Kitchen** to give you the boost for the confrontation with the shark.

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Salon
2. Bridge Room
3. Mechanical Room
4. Mess Room
5. Kitchen
6. Cabin Room
7. Supply Room
8. Meeting Room

Add more rooms if your design needs them.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Defensive Manual
2. Harpoon Gun
3. Fishing Net
4. Flashlight
5. First-Aid Kit
6. Energy Drink

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

The Rogue Shark is a powerful and unpredictable predator that has invaded the ship, throwing everything into chaos. It has now made its way into the meeting room, turning the situation into a deadly threatt. You must find a way to defeat the shark before the situation escalates.
